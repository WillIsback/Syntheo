The Syntheo beta asks a single question: **can a summary generated by Syntheo be sent without much rework of its content?** This page describes the method, the thresholds set before the first result, the data collected, and your rights. It is written for testers and for anyone evaluating the pilot.

Taking part is voluntary. Measurement is off by default and runs on your computer, without AI. Syntheo sends nothing: you hand over an export file yourself, if you choose to.

## What is established, and what is not

| Status | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Proven, outside the beta's scope** | Live transcription in French. |
| **Designed this way, outside the beta's scope** | Syntheo is designed to keep no audio and to send meetings only to the servers you set up; the static audit found no automatic flow. See [Data and privacy](/en/data-and-privacy) and [Security](/en/security). |
| **Observed once** | A summary built with Syntheo and shared after partial rework, by its designer. One user who knows the tool: this is not proof. |
| **Never checked: the purpose of the beta** | That someone else gets, on their own meetings, a sendable summary with little content rework. That adding a term to the dictionary reduces corrections of proper names and acronyms. |

## The method in plain words

### What is compared

Two versions of the same summary:

- the **generated version**, produced by Syntheo, before any edit;
- the **sent version**, the one you actually shared, after your corrections.

The difference between them is the **rework**: what you had to change before sending.

### How

The calculation is deterministic: the same texts always give the same numbers. No AI model is involved, and nothing goes over the network.

1. **Normalization.** Formatting is removed (headings, bullets, bold, links, whose text is kept). Spaces are merged and punctuation is split from words. Capitalization is kept.
2. **Splitting into sections.** The headings of the generated version split both texts. A section missing on one side counts entirely as added or deleted. If fewer than half of the headings are found in the sent version, the measurement covers the whole text, and the export says so.
3. **Word-by-word comparison.** Each difference is an addition, a deletion, or a replacement. A replacement weighs the number of words on its longer side.
4. **Classification.** A difference is **lexical** if it is a short replacement (1 to 3 words on each side) that involves a proper name, an acronym, or a term from your dictionary: for example a misspelled person's name. Everything else is **content**, including a proper name added or removed on its own: a missing name is missing information.
5. **Counters.** Only word counts are kept: words generated, added, deleted, replaced (content) and replaced (lexical), in total and per section. The texts are released immediately.

Two indicators follow, per 1,000 generated words:

- **content rework** = (added + deleted + replaced for content) ÷ generated × 1,000. This is the main indicator;
- **lexical rework** = replaced (lexical) ÷ generated × 1,000.

The two are never added together. The calculation and its outputs are detailed in the [`syntheo mesure` command reference](/en/reference/cli#mesure-command), which applies the same rule from a terminal.

### The classification is checked by hand

The lexical or content classification relies on a simple rule that can be wrong. Before the beta, at least 200 differences are classified by hand and compared with the rule. The target is at least 85% agreement; below that, the rule is fixed before the beta. The measured agreement rate is published with the results.

### In the app

Two settings, off by default, are in **Settings → Advanced → Privacy**:

- **Measure rework of summaries** adds the "Measure rework…" action to the summary menu. You paste or drop the sent version; only numbers are kept on your computer, never text.
- **Calibration mode** is used to check the classification: after a measurement, it lists each difference so you can classify it by hand. Excerpts stay on screen; only the agreement counts are kept.

Below these settings, two buttons:

- **Export my measurements…** shows the whole export file; **Save the file…** saves it where you choose. Nothing is sent.
- **Erase my measurements…**, then **Erase all measurements and my pseudonym**, erases from your computer the counters, the calibration, the term and summary tracking, the copy of generated summaries and the pseudonym, then **turns measurement off** along with Calibration mode. If you turn it back on, you start again with a new pseudonym.

A managed deployment can turn measurement off through a managed setting.

## What is measured, and what is not

Three indicators are measured:

| Indicator | Role | Read as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Content rework | Main | Per meeting, then median per tester |
| Lexical rework | Support | Per term, before and after it is added to the dictionary |
| Abandonment rate | Safeguard | Per tester: share of generated summaries not measured, exported or shared within 7 days, then median across qualified testers |

**Not** measured: the number of meetings or summaries, time spent in the app, stated satisfaction, or a score given by an AI. No score mixes content and lexical rework.

### The limits, plainly

- **This is not proof.** Participants are few, volunteers, close to one another, and not randomly selected.
- **You declare the sent version.** It may contain additions from elsewhere, and nobody can check it.
- **The lexical classification is approximate.** Its error rate is published.
- **Low rework does not prove a good summary.** It can come from a quick read: the measurement cannot see what should have been corrected and was not.
- **Correction time**, if measured later, will only cover people who correct inside Syntheo.

## Thresholds, set in advance

The thresholds below are set before the beta. **No threshold will change after the first export is received.** Any later change will be dated and published on this page, with its reason.

| Threshold | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Qualified tester | At least 5 measured meetings. Otherwise, the tester is marked "insufficient", with no figure. |
| Publishable result | At least 5 qualified testers. Otherwise, figures are given tester by tester, with no conclusion. |
| **Success** | Median content rework of 100 per 1,000 or less for at least 2 out of 3 qualified testers, **and** a median abandonment rate across qualified testers of 20% or less. |
| **Clear failure** | Median content rework above 250 per 1,000 for the majority of qualified testers. |
| Grey zone | Everything else. Reported as such. |
| Decisions and Actions safeguard | If additions exceed 50 words per 1,000 generated in the Decisions or Actions section for at least a third of the qualified testers for whom the safeguard is computable, this is flagged, even in case of success. For each tester, the value used is the median over their meetings. The safeguard is not computable for a tester with no recognized Decisions or Actions section: that tester is left out of the one-third calculation, and the result says so ("not computable for N testers"). |
| Abandonment | A generated summary that is not measured, exported, or shared within 7 days of being generated. A summary whose 7 days have not elapsed by the date of the export does not count yet. Sharing means copying the summary to the clipboard (to paste it into an email or a wiki, the usual case) or sending it through a webhook. |

For a sense of scale: 100 per 1,000 means about one word in ten of the summary was added, removed, or changed in content.

### Does the dictionary really help?

When you often correct a name, you add it to the dictionary, and then it comes back less often. But it might have come back less often anyway: a term corrected several times in a row often becomes rare again on its own (regression to the mean). To separate the two effects, the beta compares two groups of terms selected by the same rule, at the same moment:

- a term **enters the study** once it has been corrected twice;
- it is **treated** if it is added to the dictionary at the meeting where it entered or within the next 2 meetings;
- it is a **comparison** term if it is added later, or never;
- it is **excluded** if it had been added before it entered;
- for each term, its errors are counted over the 5 meetings before and the 5 meetings after it entered; the entry meeting belongs to neither window, and each side needs at least 3 meetings with generated words;
- the dictionary's effect is the drop for treated terms minus the drop for comparison terms, with an uncertainty interval.

The result is only shown with at least 20 treated and 20 comparison terms, from at least 3 testers; otherwise the result states "N terms missing" or "N testers missing". Limit: you choose which terms to add, it is not random. The starting frequency of both groups is published so readers can judge.

Terms are designated by an index (`t1`, `t2`…). The mapping between the index and the term stays on your computer and is never exported.

## The data

### On your computer

Syntheo keeps counters, identifiers, dates and, for each corrected term, a fingerprint with a local key (HMAC-SHA-256, with a key drawn at random when you join and destroyed when you erase): not the sent version, not the section headings, not the terms themselves. A copy of the summary as generated is kept on your computer while measurement is on, to compare it with the sent version; it is cleared when you turn measurement off or erase your measurements, and is never exported. For the abandonment rate, Syntheo notes each summary generation and the date of its first file export and first share (copy or webhook send), only while measurement is on: a summary generated while measurement is off is not in the export, even if measured later. A regenerated summary counts as a new summary. Turning measurement off closes the summaries in progress: a measurement or share made after turning it back on does not attach to them, and a summary closed before its 7 days without being used is not in the export, since it was not observed long enough. Only measurements compared with the summary as generated go into the export; a measurement made without that copy (compared with the already reworked summary) stays on your computer. An automated test fails if a measurement table or export contains free text.

### In the export

You export by hand. Syntheo shows you the whole file before saving it and sends nothing: you hand it over yourself, through the channel set by the organization running the pilot. The schema is closed: no field outside this list is accepted, and every text value follows a fixed format or a closed list.

**File header**

| Field | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `schema_version` | Version of the export format. | `1` |
| `app_version` | Version of Syntheo that produced the export. | `"0.8.0"` |
| `rule_version` | Version of the rework calculation rule. | `1` |
| `rubric_version` | Version of the rubric of a future AI classification; `0` as long as it does not exist. | `0` |
| `pseudonym` | Random pseudonym drawn at enrollment, erasable. It is derived from no data about the person or the computer. | `"p-3f9a1c0e7b2d4f6a8c1e3b5d7f9a2c4e"` |
| `enrolled_days` | Days elapsed between enrollment and the export. | `21` |
| `meetings` | One entry per measured summary (see below). | |
| `summaries` | One entry per generated summary, measured or not, for the abandonment rate: a summary with no measurement, export, or share within 7 days of being generated counts as abandoned. | |
| `terms` | One entry per term tracked for the dictionary study. | |

**Each measured meeting (`meetings`)**

| Field | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `meeting` | Index of the measured meeting, unrelated to its identifiers in Syntheo. | `"m1"` |
| `summary` | Index of the matching generated summary. | `"s1"` |
| `day` | Day of the measurement, counted from enrollment. | `2` |
| `mode` | How the sent version was obtained: `paste` (pasted or dropped) or `correction` (corrected inside Syntheo, later). | `"paste"` |
| `aligned` | `true` if the measurement was done section by section, `false` if it covers the whole text. | `true` |
| `headings_generated`, `headings_matched` | Number of headings in the generated version, and how many are found in the sent version. | `3`, `3` |
| `unmatched_sent_words` | Words in sections of the sent version that are missing from the generated version. | `0` |
| `total` | Counters for the whole meeting (see below). | |
| `sections` | For each section: `index` (its rank), `role` (`decisions` or `actions` according to the summary template, `other` otherwise), `matched` (found or not) and `counters` (its counters, see below). Never its heading. | `{"index": 1, "role": "decisions", "matched": true, "counters": {…}}` |

**Counters (`total` and each section's `counters`)**

| Field | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `generated` | Words in the generated version. | `28` |
| `added` | Words added. | `3` |
| `deleted` | Words deleted. | `2` |
| `replaced_content` | Words replaced for content. | `4` |
| `replaced_lexical` | Words replaced in lexical corrections. | `1` |

**Each generated summary (`summaries`)**

| Field | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `summary` | Index of the summary. | `"s1"` |
| `generated_day` | Day of generation, counted from enrollment. | `2` |
| `measured` | Day of the first measurement, or `null`. | `2` |
| `exported` | Day the note was first exported to a file (Markdown, PDF…), or `null`. | `null` |
| `shared` | Day of the first share: summary copied to the clipboard or sent through a webhook, or `null`. | `3` |

**Each tracked term (`terms`)**

| Field | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `term` | Index of the term. Never the term itself. | `"t1"` |
| `added_after` | Measured meeting after which the term was added to the dictionary, or `null`. | `"m1"` |
| `errors` | Number of corrections of the term, meeting by meeting (`meeting`, `count`). A missing meeting counts as 0. | `[{"meeting": "m1", "count": 1}]` |

**Never in the export**: no text from a meeting, summary, or sent version, no section or note heading, no dictionary term, no name of a person or participant, no real date or time (only days counted from enrollment), no identifier of the computer or an account.

<Warning>
  **The export is pseudonymized, not anonymous.** The pseudonym is random, but with few participants, someone who knows the pilot can sometimes recognize a file (by its number of meetings or its pace). The export therefore remains personal data and is handled as such.
</Warning>

The tool that gathers exports (`scripts/beta/` in the [repository](https://github.com/WillIsback/Syntheo)) treats each file as untrusted. It rejects a file that does not match the schema or whose content is inconsistent (for example a share dated before generation). For a given pseudonym, it keeps only the most recent export; if two different exports compete for that place, it rejects them.

## Your rights

- **Stop at any time**, without giving a reason: turn off "Measure rework of summaries". Nothing more is measured.
- **Erase your measurements on your computer**: **Settings → Advanced → Privacy**, **Erase my measurements…**, then confirm. The counters and the pseudonym are erased and measurement is turned off; an export already handed over cannot be taken back from your computer.
- **Ask for files already handed over to be deleted**: contact the organization running the pilot and give your pseudonym (`p-…`). It is the only way to find your files, since they do not contain your name.

Not taking part, or stopping, changes nothing in how you use Syntheo.

## A disappointing result will be published

If content rework exceeds the threshold, or if terms added to the dictionary do no better than the others, the result will be published as is, on this page, with the same prominence as a success.
