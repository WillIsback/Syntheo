On 150 utterances of read French speech, **On this computer** transcription has a word error rate of **12.55 %** (95 % interval: 10.73 to 14.45 %), about one word in eight. The int8 quantization of the shipped models causes no measurable degradation compared with the original FP32 weights (12.52 %).

These figures cover read speech, cleanly recorded. They measure neither a real meeting nor the quality of summaries: read [What these figures do not measure](#what-these-figures-do-not-measure) before comparing them with your use.

## Results by engine

The word error rate (WER) counts substituted, missing, or added words, divided by the number of words in the reference. Lower is better.

| Transcription engine | Model | WER | 95 % interval | Utterances | Words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [On this computer](/en/on-device-transcription) (`syntheo-speech`), Syntheo 0.8.0 to 0.8.2 | NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 ASR Streaming 0.6B, reduced-range int8 (shipped models) | 12.55 % | 10.73 to 14.45 % | 150 | 4,208 |
| Reference, not shipped | Same model, original FP32 weights | 12.52 % | 10.72 to 14.35 % | 150 | 4,208 |
| [Local transcription service](/en/local-transcription-service) (NVIDIA NeMo-Speech.cpp) | Nemotron 3.5 ASR Streaming 0.6B, `q8_0` quantization | Not measured | — | — | — |
| OpenAI-compatible server ([Set up transcription and AI](/en/ai-setup)) | The model you choose | Depends on the chosen model, not measured | — | — | — |

The paired difference between the shipped models and the FP32 reference is +0.02 point (95 % interval: −0.39 to +0.44): it cannot be measured with this sample. The NeMo-Speech.cpp service uses the same base model, but another quantization and another runtime: the result above does not apply to it.

## Measurement conditions

- **Date**: November 22, 2026.
- **Models**: the files of `apps/speech/models/models.json`, byte for byte identical to those Syntheo 0.8.0, 0.8.1, and 0.8.2 download. They are quantized from the `altunenes/parakeet-rs` ONNX export (revision `26684938ff242cb4f7a65fad712476c6015a4477`) of `nvidia/nemotron-3.5-asr-streaming-0.6b` (revision `ea30d66debe3740a08b573244286791d423d6b3e`). The FP32 reference is the same export, not quantized.
- **Hardware**: AMD Ryzen 9 9950X (AVX-512 with VNNI), limited to 4 cores, 4 ONNX Runtime threads.
- **Run**: each file is transcribed whole, outside real time, without the app's capture chain (microphone, system audio, echo cancellation). Diarization plays no part in the WER.
- **Audio**: 29.9 min in total, 16 kHz mono 16-bit PCM WAV.

On a CPU without VNNI, int8 arithmetic rounds differently. Measured under emulation on 16 utterances of this sample, the difference from FP32 stays within the measurement noise (see the model card, `apps/speech/models/model-card.md`).

## What these figures do not measure

- **Read speech, not a meeting**: sentences read by one person, with no hesitation, restart, or conversation.
- **No overlapping speech**: never two people at once.
- **Few accents**: the FLEURS and LibriVox readers do not represent the range of accents in a meeting.
- **Clean microphone**: clear recordings, with no room noise, echo, compressed video call, or distant microphone.
- **General vocabulary**: encyclopedic and literary texts, with no domain jargon, your organization's proper names, or acronyms (the [dictionary](/en/languages#proper-names-and-jargon) is there for that).
- **Not summaries**: WER measures transcription alone, not the quality of the summaries your LLM server writes afterwards.
- **Limited sample**: 150 utterances cannot detect a difference smaller than about 0.5 point between two variants.
- **MLS part**: it comes from the 1-hour subset of the Multilingual LibriSpeech training split. If the model was trained on MLS, which is not checked here, this part may be optimistic.

In a real meeting, expect a higher error rate.

## Method

### Sample

- 100 utterances from **FLEURS** `fr_fr`, `test` split: 100 distinct sentences drawn with `random.Random(20261122)`, then one recording per sentence.
- 50 utterances from **Multilingual LibriSpeech** `french`, `1_hours` subset: drawn with `random.Random(20261123)` among the utterances sorted by identifier.
- References are the datasets' transcripts, unchanged (for FLEURS, the raw transcription, with punctuation).

The Syntheo repository holds no audio. The [`apps/speech/wer-corpus`](https://github.com/WillIsback/Syntheo/tree/main/apps/speech/wer-corpus) folder holds the list of utterances (`utterances.tsv`: identifier, dataset, split, source file, SHA-256 checksums, reference), the sources (`sources.json`), the attribution, and the scripts.

### Normalization and scoring

Before errors are counted, references and transcripts are normalized: Unicode NFC form, lowercase, typographic apostrophes turned into `'`, apostrophes inside a word kept, other punctuation replaced with a space. Numbers are not normalized: "12" and "douze" count as one error.

WER is the total of word-level edits (substitutions, deletions, insertions) divided by the total number of reference words. The 95 % interval comes from 1,000 bootstrap resamples of the utterances with replacement (`random.Random(12345)`). The difference between two variants is paired: it is computed on the same resamples.

### Reproduce

From a checkout of the Syntheo repository, with `uv` and `ffmpeg`:

```sh
# 1. Rebuild the sample (several hundred MB downloaded from Hugging Face)
uv run --locked --script apps/speech/wer-corpus/prepare.py "$HOME/wer-fr"

# 2. Transcribe each $HOME/wer-fr/wav/<id>.wav into $HOME/hyp-int8/<id>.txt

# 3. Compute the WER and its interval
python3 -I apps/speech/wer-corpus/score.py "$HOME/wer-fr/refs.tsv" int8="$HOME/hyp-int8"
```

`prepare.py` checks the checksums of the source files, redraws the sample with the seeds, and stops if it does not give exactly the list in the repository. It also compares each WAV it produces with the measured one; another ffmpeg version may give different bytes, which it reports without stopping.

<Note>
  The bench that transcribed the files for these measurements is a local tool, not yet published in the repository: step 2 cannot yet be reproduced identically. The Hugging Face revision of FLEURS was not recorded at measurement time; `prepare.py` takes the latest one and checks the checksum of every selected file.
</Note>
