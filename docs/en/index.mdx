Syntheo is a desktop app for Linux and Windows. It captures your microphone and your computer's audio without joining the call as a bot. Each meeting becomes a living note: you prepare it, Syntheo listens, then it turns your notes into a summary (備 Prepare · 聴 Listen · 結 Synthesis). [Why tea?](/en/philosophy)

Transcription and summaries go only through the servers you configure (vLLM, Ollama, speaches, NVIDIA NeMo-Speech.cpp…). Syntheo has no account, no subscription, no telemetry, and never keeps the audio.

## At a glance

1. Configure your transcription server and your summary server in **Settings → My servers** (see [Set up transcription and AI](/en/ai-setup)).
2. On the home screen, click "Start a meeting", then "Begin listening".
3. Take quick notes while Syntheo listens: the transcript scrolls in the margin.
4. Click "Finish": Syntheo writes the summary from the transcript and your notes.
5. File the note in a folder to follow the project from one meeting to the next, and find it again with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd>, by its title or its content.

Your notes are stored locally, in a SQLite database on your computer. The audio lives in memory long enough to be sent to your transcription server, then disappears. See [Data and privacy](/en/data-and-privacy).
