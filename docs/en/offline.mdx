Syntheo needs the internet for none of its features: it has no account, no sync and no online service. What it needs is **your inference servers**. If they run on your computer or on your local network, everything works offline. If they are unreachable, the features that depend on them stop.

## What always works

These features call no server:

- creating, editing and organizing your notes and folders;
- reviewing transcripts and summaries already produced;
- full-text search (local index);
- exporting your notes;
- managing your summary templates.

## What requires the transcription server

- meeting transcription;
- dictation;
- importing an audio file.

Without a reachable transcription server, **there is no transcription**. Syntheo never keeps audio: so it cannot record now to transcribe later.

- If no transcription provider is set up, Syntheo refuses to start the capture: "No transcription provider is set up. Syntheo does not keep audio: set up a transcription endpoint before recording."
- If the server becomes unreachable during a meeting, live transcription pauses and Syntheo tries to reconnect. The passage spoken during the outage will be missing from the transcript.

## Echo cancellation models

Echo cancellation removes the other participants' voices that your microphone picks up from your speakers. Its models are not bundled with the app: Syntheo downloads them once, from GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`), the first time it starts, then keeps them on your computer.

If that first start happens offline, or behind a proxy that blocks this address, **listening still works**, without echo cancellation:

- during listening, a notice says "Echo cancellation unavailable": "The models could not be downloaded (offline?). Listening continues without it.", with a "Retry" button;
- under **Settings → My meetings → Meetings → Audio**, the "Echo cancellation" row stays visible as long as the models are missing, with a "Retry" button.

Syntheo also retries on its own, after 1 minute, then 5 and 15 minutes, then every hour. As soon as the download succeeds, echo cancellation turns on, even in the middle of a listening session. Wear headphones in the meantime: the microphone then picks up no speaker sound.

## What requires the summary server

- generating summaries;
- chatting about notes ("Ask the note");
- "Suggest sections" in the template editor.

Without a reachable summary server, your transcripts remain available: you can generate the summary once the server is back.

## Work entirely offline

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the servers on your computer or local network">
    For example speaches or vLLM for transcription, Ollama or llama.cpp server for the LLM. Download the models **before** you disconnect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Declare them in Syntheo">
    Follow [Set up transcription and AI](/en/ai-setup) with local URLs, for example `http://localhost:8000/v1` and `http://localhost:11434/v1`. Plain HTTP is accepted toward `localhost` and private addresses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test before the meeting">
    Start Syntheo once with the network on, so that it downloads the echo cancellation models. Then run a short listening session, then generate a summary. If both work with the network cut off, you are ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Apart from inference, Syntheo makes very few requests: downloading the echo cancellation models once (GitHub), showing the release notes of a version other than the installed one (GitHub) and a few interface icons. They carry no meeting content and their failure blocks nothing. Full list in [Syntheo Desktop and the GDPR](/en/conformite/rgpd).
</Note>
