Syntheo is under construction (beta). This page lists the planned work, with no committed dates. The current state of the product is described in [Architecture](/en/architecture).

Whatever form the product takes, the principles stay the same: inference only on the servers you choose, no audio kept, no required account, and no telemetry.

## Planned work

<Steps>
  <Step title="CLI">
    A command-line interface distributed with the app, to browse and use your meetings from a terminal or an agent. Today, the CLI is built from source: see [Install the CLI](/en/installation).
  </Step>
  <Step title="REST API">
    A REST API to integrate Syntheo with your tools and automations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mobile version">
    A mobile app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Web version">
    A version you can use from a browser.
  </Step>
</Steps>
