Syntheo is under construction (beta). This page shows the product's main milestones: those already shipped and those to come, with no committed dates. The current state of the product is described in [Architecture](/en/architecture).

Whatever form the product takes, the principles stay the same: inference only on the servers you choose, no audio kept, no required account, and no telemetry.

<Steps>
  <Step title="0.7.0 · Projects, common thread and folders · shipped">
    Meetings are organised in project folders, with a common thread that links the meetings on one topic and prepares the next one. See [Folders](/en/folders) and the [0.7.0 release notes](https://github.com/WillIsback/Syntheo/releases/tag/v0.7.0).
  </Step>
  <Step title="0.8.0 · Native on-device transcription · shipped">
    A transcription program shipped with the app transcribes meetings directly on the computer, with no server, when the machine allows it. See [On-device transcription](/en/on-device-transcription) and the [0.8.0 release notes](https://github.com/WillIsback/Syntheo/releases/tag/v0.8.0).
  </Step>
  <Step title="0.9.0 · Drawing mode and images · next version">
    During the meeting, a Drawing mode opens a whiteboard-style surface in the note: pen, shapes, arrows, text. You sketch a diagram, a layout or an idea by hand, without trying to make it pretty.

    When the summary is generated, the language model reads this sketch and the images pasted in the note, then redraws them cleanly in the final summary. The original sketch stays attached and viewable, and the redrawn diagram is marked "to check". This needs a vision-capable model on your summary server.
  </Step>
  <Step title="1.0.0 · Synchronised mobile version">
    A mobile app synchronised with the computer: record a call from your phone while taking the note on your computer, both linked to the same meeting.
  </Step>
</Steps>
